## 💬 What Can You Do With NtfyChat?

NtfyChat is built on a reliable messaging technology called "ntfy" (pronounced "notify"). Here are the main things you can do:

- **Send Text Messages**: Type a message and hit Enter to send it instantly.
- **Create Chat Rooms**: Set up a specific group (called a "topic") for different conversations, like "Family" or "Work Projects."
- **Receive Notifications**: Get alerts when someone sends you a message, even if the app is in the background.
- **Keep It Simple**: No accounts, no phone numbers, no email verification – just pick a name and chat.

## 🛠️ How to Use NtfyChat (Step-by-Step)

Follow these simple instructions to master NtfyChat:

1. **Open the App**: After launching, you'll see a main window with a text box.
2. **Enter Your Name**: Type your name or nickname in the "Your Name" field (if shown). This tells others who you are.
3. **Choose a Chat Topic**: In the "Topic" or "Chat Room" field, type a name for your conversation. For example, "MyBirthdayParty" or "BookClub." Everyone who uses the exact same topic name will be in the same chat room.

5. **Read Messages**: Incoming messages will appear on the screen. That's it – you're chatting!

### 💡 Pro Tip for Beginners

Always share the exact topic name (including capital letters and spaces) with the people you want to chat with. If one person types "myBirthdayParty" and another types "MyBirthdayParty," they won't see each other's messages. Use the same spelling!

### Problem: "Windows protected your PC" Warning
**Solution**: This is normal. Click "More Info," then click "Run Anyway." This only happens because the app is new and hasn't been downloaded by thousands of people yet.

### Problem: The App Won't Open
**Solution**: Make sure you extracted the ZIP file completely. Try right-clicking the ZIP and selecting "Extract All" again. If it still won't open, restart your computer and try again.

### Problem: I Can't See Messages From My Friend
**Solution**: Double-check that you both are using the exact same topic name. Also, make sure both of you are connected to the internet.

**Solution**: Hold down the `Ctrl` key on your keyboard and scroll up or down with your mouse wheel to zoom in and out.
